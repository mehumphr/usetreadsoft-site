What we look at

Most businesses we speak to take payments in three or four different ways, often through different companies.

01

Online checkout

The gateway and card processing behind your website checkout, and whether they could sit with one provider instead of two.

02

Payment links

Send customers a link by email to pay a deposit, a balance or an invoice on their own card, instead of chasing bank transfers.

03

Phone payments

Take a card payment while the customer is on the phone, without writing card numbers down.

04

Card machines

Card terminals for a shop, reception, counter or site, on the same account as your online payments.
